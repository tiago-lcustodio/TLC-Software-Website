*** TLC Software ***

Free Software, Games, Science and Imagination

About TLC Software

TLC Software is a small software and game development initiative created to explore ideas, build educational tools and develop independent games with creativity, technical curiosity and social purpose. We are based on the principles of internet democratization, free and open-source software, and the defense of national and international research and science, especially in emerging countries. Our goal is to create projects that can be studied, modified, shared and improved by anyone interested in technology, education and digital culture.

Our Ideas

Democratic Technology and Free Software

We believe that technology should not be restricted to large corporations or closed environments. Software can be a powerful tool for education, creativity and autonomy when people are allowed to understand how it works. For this reason, TLC Software values free software, open knowledge and accessible development practices.

Our software and games are planned as open-source projects, available for consultation, learning and experimentation. More than finished products, they are intended to become educational resources, allowing students, developers, researchers and curious people to analyze their structure, adapt their code and learn from their development process.

Research, Security and University Autonomy

TLC Software is also concerned with the security and autonomy of university research in Brazil and around the world. Scientific development depends on digital tools that are transparent, reliable and not completely dependent on external commercial platforms. Open and auditable technologies can strengthen academic independence, protect research data and encourage collaboration between institutions, especially in countries that seek greater technological sovereignty.

Projects in Progress

  • TLC Civ - our own version inspired by the classic Civilization-style strategy game.
  • Chronicles of Virtude - a medieval fantasy RPG set in the world of Virtude.
  • City on Your Screen - a city-building simulator inspired by games like SimCity.
  • Fast Rabbit - a simple platform game where you control the Fast Rabbit.
  • Ship War - a simple spaceship game where you are a fugitive who must save the galaxy.

These projects will gradually be updated with progress reports, news, development notes and repository links as they evolve.
